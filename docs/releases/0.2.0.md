## Keep your account

Stoop has no server, so until now a reinstall or a new phone meant starting over with an empty address book.
Settings now has an **Account & backup** section, on the computer and the phone:

- **Move to another device.** Your old device shows a link and QR code; open it on the new one (same Wi-Fi) and
  your identity, contacts, profile, settings and, if you want, message history come across. The link works once
  and closes after 10 minutes.
- **Backup file.** Save the whole account as one `.stoopbackup` file, locked with a passphrase you choose, and
  restore it on any computer or phone. A phone backup restores on a computer and the other way round.
- **Recovery phrase on the computer too.** Show it, copy it, or restore from it (or from recovery shares).

Restoring never deletes what it replaces: the old account is moved into a `replaced-<time>` folder, and Stoop
restarts with the restored one.
